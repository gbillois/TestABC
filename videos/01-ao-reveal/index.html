Shot list

Techniques under test

Editing

  • 13 hard cuts locked to a 24 fps grid
  • Cut-on-impact, shutter and invert flashes
  • Whip-pan smear and a sneaker foreground wipe
  • Speed ramp: 25% slow-mo then snap-zoom

Graphics

  • Kinetic type: punch-in, spring squash, 3D text ring, 180° rotation
  • Split-screen triptych with stroboscopic echoes
  • Flat-colour shatter (radial and grid shards)
  • Halftone, barcodes, UI ticks, target-lock HUD, RGB split

Character

  • Procedural 2D rig with 17 key poses, blended per frame
  • One model reused everywhere (full colour, silhouettes, ghosts)
  • Close-up face and eye drawings matched to the rig design

Sound

Web Audio synth at 120 BPM: kick, hats and bass on the grid, and every cut, slam, shatter and stamp gets its own hit. The same schedule renders offline into the MP4's audio track.
