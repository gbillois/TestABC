Notes

  • Everything on screen — the character, the typography, the shards — is drawn procedurally per frame; every frame is a pure function of its frame number, so the film scrubs exactly and can be exported frame-by-frame later (window.AO_FILM.renderFrame(n), or open ?f=120 for a still).
  • Cut timings follow the brief, with decimal seconds snapped to the nearest 24 fps frame (e.g. 3.10 s → frame 74).
  • The brief was cut off at CUT 12 | 11.10-13.00s. Cuts 12 and 13 are a proposal: a hero landing with outfit call-outs, then an end-card lockup that returns to the opening frame so the loop closes.
  • The synth beat is optional and off by default.
